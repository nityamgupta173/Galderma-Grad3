The post-procedure window

Where photoprotection changes the result

Fillers, biostimulators, peels and resurfacing all end with the same instruction: stay out of the sun. On Fitzpatrick IV–VI skin, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation is the complication that actually presents, and it is largely preventable — which makes photoprotection part of the result rather than aftercare advice.
