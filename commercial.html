Why the tool holds up commercially

Three properties that matter at scale

It costs nothing to run

The whole experience is static, so serving it is effectively free and it absorbs traffic spikes without provisioning. That matters for a geofenced campaign, where demand arrives in bursts tied to the UV index rather than evenly across a month.

One model, five pages

Every page reads the same six inputs and calls the same function, so the site cannot contradict itself. Change the city on the diagnostic and the degradation curve re-stresses to match — the consistency is structural, not manual.

Nothing leaves the browser

Inputs persist for the session only and nothing is transmitted. For a tool that asks about skin conditions, collecting nothing is the correct default — and it removes consent and data-residency friction from the launch path entirely.
