Why the dose pump changes the economics

!The problem. People apply 0.7 mg/cm². SPF is tested at 2.0. So an SPF 50 pack delivers roughly SPF 18. Every brand in India sells a number its own users never receive.
✓The fix. A calibrated actuator gives exactly 1.0 ml per stroke — the validated face-and-neck dose. Two strokes. No mirror, no guessing, no reformulation.
↻The flywheel. Correct dosing cuts pack life from ~110 days to 45. Eight refills a year instead of three. The honest choice and the commercial choice are the same one.
