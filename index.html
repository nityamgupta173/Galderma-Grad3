Where the damage is coming from

Sunscreen is tested against ultraviolet light. But visible light — ordinary light you can see, from screens, ceiling lights and windows — also triggers melanin production in brown skin. Standard SPF does not block it. Only a mineral tint containing iron oxides does. That is why this slider matters, and why one product in the range is tinted.
