Why the dose pump changes the economics

!The problem. People apply about 0.7 mg/cm². SPF is certified at 2.0. So an SPF 50 pack delivers roughly SPF 18, and every brand in India is selling a number its own users never receive.
✓The fix. A calibrated actuator dispenses exactly 1.0 ml per stroke — the validated face-and-neck dose. Two strokes. No mirror, no guessing, and no reformulation, so it costs tooling rather than R&D.
↻The flywheel. Correct dosing cuts pack life from about 110 days to 45. Eight refills a year instead of three. We are aware the honest recommendation is also the commercially favourable one — that is the point, not a coincidence.
